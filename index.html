This policy covers the FreeFrame Android app distributed by Hit Plane Studio and this privacy-policy webpage. Photo cleanup works on your device. Optional support payments use Google Play. Those are separate activities, with different data handling explained below.

1. Photo and video access

FreeFrame reads the local photos and videos that Android makes available under the permissions you grant. It reads media identifiers, file names, album or folder names, dates taken or added, file sizes and video durations to display previews and organise your library. Image decoding can read dimensions and orientation information to display a preview correctly.

This information supports month and album browsing, Recents, Random, On This Day, screenshots, videos, bookmarks and your keep/remove choices. Exact-duplicate photo scanning compares file sizes and calculates SHA-256 hashes from photo contents on your device. It does not send those hashes or files to a server.

The app's photo-cleanup code does not upload your photos, videos, library metadata, review choices or duplicate comparisons to Hit Plane Studio, Google billing, or another cloud service. It does not provide cloud-library synchronisation. Only media Android makes available locally is included; a separate gallery or photo-backup service has its own behaviour and policy.

2. Permissions and your choices

  • Photos and videos on Android 13 and newer. READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO provide the photo/video access you allow.
  • Selected photos on Android 14 and newer. READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED supports access limited to items you select.
  • Earlier Android versions. READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is limited to Android 12L and earlier. WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is limited to Android 9 and earlier and supports user-confirmed local removal.
  • Optional Google Play payments. com.android.vending.BILLING enables checkout. The merged billing dependencies also declare INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for their network operations.
  • AndroidX internal receiver protection. The app-specific DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION is a signature-level permission used by a supporting AndroidX library. It is not photo, location or advertising access.

FreeFrame does not request all-files access, location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising-ID permission. You can deny or change photo/video access through Android Settings. Limited access restricts what the app can display or manage; denying it does not prevent you from reading this policy in Settings.

3. Local storage and retention

FreeFrame saves onboarding completion, keep/remove decisions linked to local media identifiers, bookmarks, removed-item counts and sizes, and activity dates used for streaks. These are saved in the app's private Android preferences so you can resume. They do not have an automatic expiry period in the app.

Library lists and duplicate-comparison results are processed in memory. Android, WebView and supporting libraries can also maintain temporary previews, caches or diagnostic storage. FreeFrame does not maintain a developer-hosted copy of your library or sorting progress.

The app also stores a local flag recording whether you have opened Donate. Billing is initialised only after that first visit and can reconnect on later foreground visits to finish pending payments. Product details, purchase state, purchase tokens and acknowledgement/consumption results are processed for that purpose; purchase tokens are not saved in FreeFrame's preferences or passed to the photo interface.

The app sets android:allowBackup="false" to disable Android cloud app backup. Device-to-device transfer behaviour can still vary by Android version and device manufacturer. FreeFrame has no separate backup or sync service.

4. Deletion, reset and recovery

A left swipe marks an item for review. It does not delete the file. FreeFrame removes only the items you review and explicitly confirm. On Android 11 and newer, it requests Android's system trash, with system confirmation. Recovery and retention depend on the device and gallery; this app does not set a universal trash-retention period. On Android 8–10, confirmed local removal is permanent and FreeFrame shows an additional warning.

  • Reset sorting progress clears keep/remove decisions. It preserves bookmarks, removal statistics, activity dates and the local billing-enabled flag.
  • Clear storage in Android Settings or uninstall FreeFrame to remove the app's locally saved progress and preferences. Clearing app storage does not itself delete your original gallery media.
  • Google purchases, support emails and website logs are held separately. Uninstalling FreeFrame does not delete those records. Use the relevant service's controls or contact the studio about correspondence you supplied.

FreeFrame cannot recover permanently deleted originals or remotely erase information held only on your phone. Your separately configured photo-backup services may retain copies under their own settings and policies.

5. Optional donations through Google Play

Every app feature remains available without a donation. There is no donation subscription, paid feature, badge or other reward. Available amounts and local currency come from Google Play. You choose an amount and must confirm Google's checkout before a payment is made.

Google handles the account and payment method used for a donation. FreeFrame does not receive your card or bank-account details. It receives product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase package/state information and billing results so it can finish a support payment and allow another voluntary payment later. Pending purchases are not treated as completed.

Google may make order and transaction information available to Hit Plane Studio through Play Console for payment administration, refunds and related records. Google's processing, security and retention are governed by Google's privacy policy and its payment-service terms. The app does not implement a fixed retention period for Google's records. Purchase history and refund controls are available through Google Play.

6. Third-party libraries and network processing

This release includes Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0. Its resolved dependencies include AndroidX, Kotlin/coroutines, Google Play services (including base, basement, tasks and location components), Google DataTransport and Firebase encoding libraries. Firebase encoding libraries serialise data; their presence is not a Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration.

The billing library and its supporting components can communicate with Google for product availability, transaction processing and billing diagnostics. Billing requests identify the app and relevant products or transactions. Google's services may process account, app/device, identifier and diagnostic information under Google's privacy policy. FreeFrame's local media and sorting data are not attached to these requests by the app.

FreeFrame does not call the bundled location APIs or request location permission. No advertising SDK, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration, developer-operated analytics service, app-account system or cloud-storage integration is configured in this app. This is not a claim that Google's billing services collect no data.

Google's components may retain service records or queue diagnostics separately from FreeFrame's preferences. Their full server-side processing and retention are controlled by Google, rather than by the local photo-cleanup code.

7. Sharing with other apps and services

If you choose Open original, FreeFrame gives a viewing app temporary read access to that selected media item through Android. The viewer you choose and any services it uses follow their own privacy practices. Opening an original does not give that viewer the whole FreeFrame progress database.

Android, WebView, Google Play, your device manufacturer and any separately configured backup or gallery service may process information under their own policies. FreeFrame has no developer-operated media server or advertising-sharing feature. Information you separately send to support or use for a Google Play payment is handled as described in this policy.

8. Security

FreeFrame uses Android's permission controls and app-private storage. The bundled interface accepts only its approved local files and local media previews; external navigation and file/content URL access are blocked in that WebView. The release disables Android debugging and cleartext network traffic.

Saved preferences are not protected by a separate app-level encryption system or password. Their protection depends on Android's storage sandbox, your device's security and operating-system settings. No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Optional Google payments and support email have their own providers' security controls.

9. Support correspondence and privacy requests

If you voluntarily email hitplanestudio@gmail.com, Hit Plane Studio receives your address, message and any attachments you send. This support correspondence is handled through email providers and is separate from the app's on-device storage. Please avoid sending private photos or unnecessary sensitive information.

You can contact the studio about access, correction or deletion of information you supplied to support, and ask about its retention. There is no automatic email-deletion schedule implemented in FreeFrame; mailbox and transaction records may need to be retained for relevant correspondence, disputes or applicable record-keeping obligations. The studio cannot use an email request to access or erase media or progress held only on your device.

10. This webpage and GitHub Pages

The policy available through the app's Settings is bundled in the app and can be read offline. If you visit the public copy on GitHub Pages, your browser connects to GitHub. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security, including visitors who are not signed in. Other hosting/request information is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

This policy page contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, tracking pixels, external fonts or third-party media embeds. Its own code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Following an external policy or email link takes you to that separate service. This webpage is not a mechanism for uploading your Android media library.

11. Children and policy changes

FreeFrame is a general-purpose library utility. It does not ask for age or create user profiles. Contact Hit Plane Studio with privacy questions about a child's use; Google's separate account and payment controls apply when Google services are used.

If the app's data handling changes, the published and bundled policy should be updated with a revised effective date. This policy describes the functionality and bundled libraries in the current FreeFrame release, rather than possible future advertising or services.
